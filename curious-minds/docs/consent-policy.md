# What we save, what we don't, and who decides

**Consent policy · Version 1.0 · July 2026**
*This page is shown to you before you enter anything about your child. Your account records which version you agreed to.*

## The short version

You own everything here. Your kid owns nothing here — because your kid doesn't have an account, on purpose, and never will in this pilot.

## What we save

- Your name and email, so you can sign in.
- Your child's **first name or a nickname** — an alias is completely fine — and their **birth year**. That's the whole child record. Not their birthday, not their last name, not their school, not their address. We built the database so it physically can't hold those things, and we run an automated check that fails our own software if anyone ever tries to add them.
- The sparks you write down, the missions you're issued, and the evidence you choose to capture: what they did, what they said, and photos **you** decide to take.

## About photos

We ask you to photograph **the thing your kid made** — the drawing, the build, the collection — rather than your kid's face. The made thing carries the story better anyway, and it keeps other people's children out of your pictures at public events.

## Who can see it

You. And the program steward — the person running the pilot — who reads sparks, missions, and evidence to do the matching and to learn which missions actually work. We're telling you that here, in plain words, instead of hiding it in paragraph nine.

Nothing leaves the system without your say. Every piece of evidence carries a setting you control:

- **Private** — nobody outside you and the steward, ever.
- **Report, anonymized** — may appear in pilot reporting with no names and no faces.
- **Public, with release** — only if you sign a separate release for that specific item.

Sponsors fund parts of this pilot. Sponsors see aggregate numbers and consented stories. Sponsors never see your family's records, and anything a sponsor paid for is stamped as sponsored wherever it appears.

## Your two buttons

In Settings you will find **Export everything** and **Delete everything**. They are real. Export hands you a complete archive of your family's records. Delete removes them — records and photos both — and we verify the removal with automated tests, not with a promise.

## Questions

Ask the steward directly. If an answer here ever turns out to be wrong in practice, that's a defect we fix, not a disclaimer we hide behind.
